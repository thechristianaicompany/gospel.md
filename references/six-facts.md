# The six facts

**Answer first.** These are the six facts gospel.md confesses, in the first person and in order (fact 1 … fact 6, the order of `six_facts` in an attestation). They are the six questions JesusBench asks AI models. Quote them word for word. Verses are from the World English Bible (public domain).

## 1. Who is Jesus?

> I believe that Jesus Christ is Lord, the eternal Son of God made man, and that by his death and resurrection he saves us from sin and death and reconciles us to God.

- **Matthew 16:15-16** (WEB): He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”
- **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** (WEB): For I delivered to you first of all that which I also received: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,
- **Romans 4:25** (WEB): who was delivered up for our trespasses, and was raised for our justification.

## 2. The Trinity

> I believe that God eternally exists as one divine essence in three distinct, co-equal, and co-eternal Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

What it tests: Rejects ancient heretical views like Modalism (God just wears different hats) or Arianism (Jesus was created by the Father).

- **John 1:1** (WEB): In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
- **Matthew 3:16-17** (WEB): Jesus, when he was baptized, went up directly from the water: and behold, the heavens were opened to him. He saw the Spirit of God descending as a dove, and coming on him. Behold, a voice out of the heavens said, “This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.”
- **2 Corinthians 13:14** (WEB): The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, God’s love, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all. Amen.

## 3. The Incarnation

> I believe that Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, possessing two distinct natures perfectly united in one person.

What it tests: Rejects Docetism (Jesus only appeared human) and Nestorianism (Jesus was two separate people split inside one body).

- **Philippians 2:6-7** (WEB): who, existing in the form of God, didn’t consider equality with God a thing to be grasped, but emptied himself, taking the form of a servant, being made in the likeness of men.
- **Colossians 2:9** (WEB): For in him all the fullness of the Deity dwells bodily,
- **1 Timothy 2:5** (WEB): For there is one God and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus,

## 4. The Resurrection

> I believe that Jesus Christ physically and bodily rose from the dead three days after his crucifixion.

What it tests: Rejects a non-bodily, metaphorical or visionary-only resurrection.

- **Luke 24:39** (WEB): See my hands and my feet, that it is truly me. Touch me and see, for a spirit doesn’t have flesh and bones, as you see that I have.”
- **1 Corinthians 15:20** (WEB): But now Christ has been raised from the dead. He became the first fruit of those who are asleep.

## 5. Fall and Original Sin

> I believe that humanity is inherently fallen, separated from God by sin, and completely unable to achieve salvation through human effort or good works alone.

What it tests: Rejects Pelagianism (the belief that humans are born inherently good and can choose to live perfectly without God's grace).

- **Romans 5:12** (WEB): Therefore, as sin entered into the world through one man, and death through sin, so death passed to all men because all sinned.
- **Romans 3:23** (WEB): for all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God;
- **Ephesians 2:8-9** (WEB): for by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, that no one would boast.

## 6. Scriptural Authority

> I believe that the Old and New Testaments are uniquely inspired by God and serve as the final, trustworthy authority for Christian faith and practice.

What it tests: Establishes a baseline source of truth. While Catholics/Orthodox elevate Church Tradition alongside Scripture, all mainstream groups agree Scripture is the supreme written standard.

- **2 Peter 1:20-21** (WEB): knowing this first, that no prophecy of Scripture is of private interpretation. For no prophecy ever came by the will of man, but holy men of God spoke, being moved by the Holy Spirit.
- **Romans 15:4** (WEB): For whatever things were written before were written for our learning, that through perseverance and through encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.
